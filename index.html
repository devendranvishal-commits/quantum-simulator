Quantum Computer Simulator & Molecular Docking

Two from-scratch technical demos, running live in your browser — built by Vishal, Ohio State University

No servers, no external quantum/chem libraries — the math runs client-side in JavaScript.

🧬 Molecular Docking

What this is Statevector simulator

A genuine quantum computer simulator built from scratch: qubits, gate matrices (Hadamard, RX, RY, RZZ, etc.), state-vector evolution, and measurement — all implemented directly with linear algebra, no Qiskit. Below it runs QAOA (Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm), a real hybrid quantum-classical algorithm, to solve a small Max-Cut problem: splitting delivery stops between two trucks to balance their workload.

Truck 1    Truck 2    — dashed route = split between trucks (a "cut" route)

What this is Lennard-Jones potential

A simplified molecular docking simulator: candidate "drug" molecules are rotated and translated against a fixed protein binding pocket, scored using the Lennard-Jones potential (a real, standard approximation used in computational chemistry), and ranked by predicted binding strength — the same core idea behind early-stage computational drug discovery (AutoDock, Glide, etc.), simplified here to run instantly in-browser.
